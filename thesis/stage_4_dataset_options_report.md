### Candidate A: Standard Bank / Xente Digital Financial Services Dataset
* **Dataset Name:** Standard Bank Tech Impact Challenge / Xente Credit Scoring
* **Source:** Zindi Africa / Standard Bank / Xente Financial Technologies
* **URL:** [https://zindi.africa/competitions/standard-bank-tech-impact-challenge](https://zindi.africa/competitions/standard-bank-tech-impact-challenge)
* **Country:** Uganda (Sub-Saharan Africa)
* **Number of Observations:** 95,662 transactions across 4,200 unique account holders
* **Variables:** `TransactionId`, `BatchId`, `AccountId`, `SubscriptionId`, `CustomerId`, `CurrencyCode`, `CountryCode`, `ProviderId`, `ProductId`, `ProductCategory` (airtime, financial services, utility bills, data bundles), `ChannelId` (web, mobile, USSD), `Amount`, `Value`, `TransactionStartTime`, `PricingStrategy`, `FraudResult` / `DefaultStatus`
* **Target Variable:** Binary outcome (`0` = Successful settlement / Non-default, `1` = Defaulted transaction / Chargeback failure)
* **Data Format:** Comma-Separated Values (CSV)
* **Licence / Usage Restrictions:** Academic Competition License. Re-distribution restricted; permitted for university academic research with proper citation.
* **Personal-Data Concerns:** High privacy compliance; customer IDs and account numbers are SHA-256 hashed. Zero PII.
* **Missing-Data Issues:** Near zero missingness (<0.01% in categorical attributes); timestamps and financial transaction amounts are 100% complete.
* **Relevance to Namibia:** Exceptionally high. Captures real-world micro-transactions across airtime, utility bill recharges, and mobile money peer-to-peer transfers, exactly mirroring the digital breadcrumbs generated by unbanked Namibians on platforms like MTC Maris and FNB eWallet.
* **Potential Limitations:** Originates in East Africa (Uganda); currency denominated in Ugandan Shillings (UGX); captures transaction-level credit rather than multi-month SME installment loans.
* **Recommended Use:** **Primary Empirical Benchmark Engine** for Objective 1, Objective 2, and Objective 3 (evaluating behavioral feature engineering, algorithmic discrimination, and probability calibration on authentic African digital finance data).

### Candidate B: Home Credit Default Risk (Thin-File & Alternative Data)
* **Dataset Name:** Home Credit Default Risk
* **Source:** Home Credit Group / Kaggle
* **URL:** [https://www.kaggle.com/c/home-credit-default-risk](https://www.kaggle.com/c/home-credit-default-risk)
* **Country:** International (Multi-country Emerging Markets)
* **Number of Observations:** 307,511 primary loan applications in training split; over 10 million relational transaction records across 7 supplementary files
* **Variables:** 122 application attributes including demographic features (`CODE_GENDER`, `DAYS_BIRTH`, `FLAG_OWN_CAR`, `NAME_EDUCATION_TYPE`), financial attributes (`AMT_INCOME_TOTAL`, `AMT_CREDIT`, `AMT_ANNUITY`), external alternative data scores (`EXT_SOURCE_1`, `EXT_SOURCE_2`, `EXT_SOURCE_3` derived from telecommunications and transactional proxies), and previous credit history
* **Target Variable:** `TARGET` (Binary: `0` = Loan repaid on schedule, `1` = Client experienced payment difficulties / 90+ days delinquency)
* **Data Format:** Relational CSVs
* **Licence / Usage Restrictions:** Open academic/competition use under Kaggle terms.
* **Personal-Data Concerns:** Fully anonymized; client identifiers are synthetic indices.
* **Missing-Data Issues:** Substantial missingness on housing/building features (>50%), but core alternative scores and applicant financial profiles are well-populated.
* **Relevance to Namibia:** High structural relevance: specifically designed for **unbanked and thin-file individuals who lack formal banking records**, enabling direct testing of how alternative proxy scores substitute for traditional bureau data.
* **Potential Limitations:** Global scope rather than African-specific; feature names for alternative data (`EXT_SOURCE`) are pre-normalized and anonymized, limiting direct raw feature inspectability.
* **Recommended Use:** Ideal secondary benchmark for testing complex gradient boosting architectures, calibration curves, and subgroup fairness audits (SRQ 2, SRQ 3, and SRQ 4).

## 2. Formal Dataset Gap Analysis (Prompt Section 6)

### Does a Sufficiently Appropriate Namibian Dataset Exist?
**No single, unified public dataset exists that satisfies all requirements for training an alternative-data credit risk machine learning model in Namibia.**

### What Exactly is Missing?
To train, benchmark, and audit a supervised machine learning credit scoring model, four distinct data elements must coincide within the same dataset:
1. **Granular alternative digital transactional logs** (mobile money transfers, airtime recharges, utility payments).
2. **True post-disbursement repayment outcomes** (binary ground-truth default labels: $Y=1$ for default, $Y=0$ for on-time repayment).
3. **Protected demographic attributes** (gender, age, regional location) to conduct algorithmic fairness audits.
4. **Namibian sovereign jurisdiction** (originating from Namibian consumers and network providers).

**The Reality in Namibia:**
* **Bank of Namibia & NSA records:** Possess authentic Namibian jurisdiction and demographics (Element 4 & Element 3), but contain **zero individual loan repayment logs or transaction microdata** (Missing Elements 1 & 2).
* **Commercial banks (First National Bank, Standard Bank, Bank Windhoek, Nedbank):** Possess loan repayment logs, but their records are strictly traditional (pay slips, formal bureau files), and are heavily protected under the *Banking Institutions Act* and common-law banking secrecy.
* **Fintech & Mobile Network Operators (MTC Maris, Letshego Tamba Maris):** Possess mobile wallet logs and microlending outcomes, but these are **strictly proprietary trade secrets** operating under confidential commercial partnerships and regulatory sandbox non-disclosure covenants.

### The Academically Defensible Solution
To overcome this empirical barrier without fabricating data or claiming access to non-existent public files, the thesis must combine real verified data with rigorous, transparent econometric calibration.
